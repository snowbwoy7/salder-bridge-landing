Privacy information

Privacy Notice

This notice describes the information handled when you use the Salder Bridge review landing page.

Information you submit

If you submit the case-review form, the information you enter—including your name, email address, selected concern category, and message—is sent through the Web3Forms form service to the recipient configured for Salder Bridge. Salder Bridge uses this information to receive and respond to your enquiry. Do not send passwords, wallet recovery phrases, private keys, full payment-card details, or sensitive identity documents through the form.

Optional website analytics

Google Analytics loads only after you explicitly select “Allow analytics” and while that saved choice remains accepted. If enabled, Google Analytics may process technical and usage information such as page views, interactions, browser and device details, and information about how you arrived at the page. The landing page's custom analytics event for a successful enquiry sends only a generic event name and form label; it does not send your name, email address, or message contents.

You can reject analytics or withdraw a previous choice later using “Privacy & analytics settings” on the landing page. When you reject or withdraw consent, the page disables further Google Analytics measurement from that point forward. Information already sent to Google before your choice changed cannot be recalled through this page.

Third-party services

The enquiry form uses Web3Forms to process submissions. Optional analytics uses Google Analytics. Please review the providers' privacy information for details about their processing:

  • Web3Forms Privacy Policy
  • Google Privacy Policy

Questions about an enquiry

For questions about an enquiry you submitted or how Salder Bridge handles it, contact support@salderbridge.com. Please do not include passwords, recovery phrases, private keys, or payment credentials in follow-up messages.
