Common questions

What to know before enquiring

Can you guarantee that my money will be recovered?

No responsible initial review can promise a particular outcome. The options available depend on the facts, records, timing, and circumstances of each case. Ask the team to explain what it can and cannot do before you proceed.

What information should I provide?

Start with a short account of what happened, the platform or broker involved, and the approximate timeline. Do not include passwords, wallet recovery phrases, private keys, payment credentials, or sensitive identity documents in an initial enquiry.

How will I know the cost and next steps?

Before agreeing to a service, ask for a clear explanation of the proposed work, applicable fees, expected process, and any limitations. Make sure you understand the terms before deciding how to proceed.
