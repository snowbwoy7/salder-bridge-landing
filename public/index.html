Does an initial case review guarantee recovery?

No. An inquiry or review cannot guarantee that funds will be recovered. Any assessment depends on the circumstances and the options actually available.

What should I include in my first inquiry?

Provide only a brief summary and a way to contact you. Do not include passwords, wallet seed phrases, private keys, payment credentials, or sensitive documents.
